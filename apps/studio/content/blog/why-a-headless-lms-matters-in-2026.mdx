## The Problem With Traditional LMS Platforms

Let’s be honest: most LMS tools feel like software from another decade.

The issues are familiar:

1. **Rigid templates** — you can't design your own learning experience.
2. **Limited customization** — small details take too much effort or are simply impossible.

4. **Branding restrictions** — you always end up looking like every other LMS.

6. **Not built for mobile-first learning** — the world has changed, but they haven’t.

We heard these frustrations repeatedly from creators, startups, schools, and enterprise teams. Everyone wanted more freedom — without losing the structure of an LMS.

That’s the gap Docento fills.

## What Makes a Headless LMS So Powerful?

A **headless LMS** separates the backend (courses, users, content, progress tracking) from the frontend (website, app, interface).

This means:

- Your learning experience can look exactly the way you want.
- You can deliver content anywhere — React app, mobile app, dashboard, or even embedded in another product.
- You don’t have to fight the LMS. It becomes a backend service, not a UI you’re forced to adopt.

This flexibility is why many developers say headless systems are the “future of learning platforms.”
