This guide will help you get started with Docento quickly.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- An Docento account (sign up at the main dashboard)
- Basic familiarity with learning management systems

## Quick Setup

### 1. Create Your First Project

Projects in Docento are containers for your courses. To create a project:

1. Navigate to the dashboard
2. Click **Create Project**
3. Enter a project name and description
4. Click **Create**

### 2. Add a Course

Once your project is created, add a course:

1. Open your project
2. Click **Add Course**
3. Fill in the course details:
   - **Title** – The name of your course
   - **Description** – A brief overview of what the course covers
   - **Target Audience** – Who this course is for
4. Click **Create Course**

### 3. Structure Your Content

Organize your course into modules and lessons:

- **Modules** – Major sections or chapters of your course
- **Lessons** – Individual learning units within modules

### 4. Upload Video Content

Docento supports video hosting with:

- Automatic transcoding for optimal streaming
- Multiple quality levels (adaptive bitrate)
- AI-powered transcription
