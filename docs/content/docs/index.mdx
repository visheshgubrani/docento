# Welcome to Docento

> Docento is a headless LMS that gives you a powerful backend and APIs while you own the learner experience.

Docento helps teams build modern learning products without being locked into a rigid, pre-built UI. You design the frontend, workflows, and brand. Docento handles the infrastructure: users, content, progress, media, and analytics.

## What is a headless LMS?

A headless LMS separates the backend learning engine from the frontend experience. With Docento, you can:

- Build a custom web, mobile, or embedded learning experience
- Integrate learning into your existing product or portal
- Keep full control over UI, content flows, and business logic
- Scale safely with multi-tenant, API-first infrastructure

## What Docento provides

- **Courses & lessons** – structured content, modules, and assessments
- **Learner management** – enrollments, cohorts, roles, and access control
- **Progress tracking** – completions, checkpoints, and analytics
- **Media delivery** – video uploads, streaming, and transcripts
- **Certificates & outcomes** – proof of completion and reporting
- **API surface** – clean endpoints for building your own frontend
